About Nepal

Nepal is a land of towering Himalayas, vibrant festivals, and diverse communities. From Kathmandu’s temples to the rural villages in the Annapurna region, Nepal blends natural beauty with warm hospitality.

Culture & festivals

  • Dashain — Nepal’s largest festival, celebrating victory, family, and new beginnings.
  • Tihar — The festival of lights, honoring animals, sisters, and the bonds of family.
  • Food — Enjoy dal bhat, momo, sel roti, and the flavors of Nepali home cooking.

Learn a few Nepali phrases

  • Namaste — Hello / Greetings
  • Dhanyabad — Thank you
  • Timro kasto cha? — How are you?
